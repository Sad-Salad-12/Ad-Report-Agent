# Narrative plan

## Audience and objective

This fully synthetic demonstration deck is for paid-media and ecommerce stakeholders. It illustrates the weekly reporting workflow without retaining any source brand, product, account, campaign, creative, audience, keyword, date, metric, filename, hash, URL, or image.

## Narrative arc and slide list

1. Cover — BRAND A, synthetic reporting period, and REGION A.
2. Overall — current-versus-previous performance, daily trend, and three-product comparison.
3. PRODUCT ALPHA — generic campaign and creative analysis with an unbranded lawn-care robot visual.
4. PRODUCT BETA — generic campaign and creative analysis with an unbranded floor-care robot visual.
5. PRODUCT GAMMA — generic campaign and creative analysis with an unbranded window-care robot visual.
6. Traffic — fictional campaign traffic efficiency.
7. Audience — fictional audience-group comparison.
8. Keywords — fictional, generalized paid-search terms.

## Source and privacy plan

All visible values are generated synthetic demonstration data. The deck contains no source filenames, hashes, account identifiers, URLs, customer information, original campaign text, or historical business metrics. Market and region are replaced by MARKET A and REGION A.

## Visual system

Use a clean white-and-navy analytical layout with a small geometric `A` corner mark. Replace all source visuals with four text-free, logo-free generated images. Keep tables and chart labels restrained and presentation-ready.

## Editability plan

All titles, labels, metrics, tables, and charts remain editable PowerPoint objects. All fourteen analytical charts remain native PowerPoint charts. Only the four generic product visuals are raster images.
